## What about multiple connections?

This is where household viewing changes the calculation.

TiviLive's FAQ explains that one connection means one simultaneous stream. Two connections allow two screens to stream at the same time, and the pricing page continues upward to five connections.

For example, the current 12-month pricing is $100 for one connection, $110 for two, $120 for three, $130 for four, and $140 for five.

That means the connection count is not just a technical setting. It is something to think about before checkout.

A single viewer watching a bedroom TV does not need five simultaneous streams.

A family where someone is watching sports in the living room while another person has a movie running upstairs is a different story.

## What should you look for in an IPTV subscription?

Price is only one part of the decision.

When comparing IPTV subscription options, look at the things that change your day-to-day experience: simultaneous connections, device compatibility, EPG support, streaming quality, trial terms, refund terms, and how the service performs during the hours you actually watch.

Recent IPTV comparison coverage also emphasizes that headline channel counts alone are not enough to judge a subscription; effective monthly cost, connection limits, trial terms, and real-world playback are more useful comparison points.

In other words, “28,000+ channels” sounds impressive, but the channel you want at 8:30 p.m. is still the one that matters.
